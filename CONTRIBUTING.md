# Contributing

This project welcomes corrections and improvements to mathematical explanations, diagrams, accessibility, and translation.

1. Identify the chapter and the exact issue.
2. For mathematical corrections, provide the original equation, the proposed correction, and a derivation or reputable reference.
3. Test the interactive graph by changing parameters and checking the geometric invariants.
4. Check right-to-left text, mathematical formula direction, and the mobile layout.
5. Avoid adding author names, historical thesis claims, or institution endorsements to the public-facing pages.

Before accepting a contribution, confirm its copyright/licence compatibility.
